## Infrastructure inventory

- Next.js App Router, React, static prerendered pages, checked-in local media, GitHub `main` → Vercel automatic deployment → custom HTTPS domain.
- No app-owned database, authentication, payment flow, enquiry API, upload handler, Server Actions or secret-dependent backend was found. Hosting account permissions, DNS ownership and private Vercel account settings are outside this source/public-response audit.
- 421 original baseline public files, about 234.8 MiB total. The largest files are project films (up to 20.71 MiB); they are on-demand media, not the initial page transfer. Original deliverables and PDFs remain intact.
- Local WebP variants use content hashes and immutable caching. HTML revalidates; no attempt to cache mutable files forever.
- No application analytics/advertising scripts, localStorage, sessionStorage or application cookie writes were found in the reviewed source. Hosting can still process operational requests/logs; this is not a claim that a hosting provider processes no personal data.

## Visual decisions

The audit supports refinement rather than a new redesign. Preserve the six floating projects, paired peripheral blur, rotated hover labels, sparse neutral surface, faded moving brand rail and existing content hierarchy. Do not add decorative dashboard widgets, giant glass panes or a heavy refraction effect. Content cards remain readable and stable; small controls carry the material and interaction feedback. Legal text uses a restrained reading column with an index, instead of putting every paragraph in another card.

## Remaining limits

- The owner explicitly removed the ribbon’s pause button. Reduced motion and offscreen pausing remain; a continuously moving ribbon without a general pause/stop/hide mechanism remains a manual WCAG 2.2.2 concern. Automated axe scores do not resolve it.
- No claim of complete captions/audio-description coverage for advertising films: source transcripts and owner-approved accessible alternatives have not been supplied. Native controls, project descriptions and reduced-motion posters are present.
- CSP permits inline scripts/styles to preserve static Next rendering. It constrains sources, frames and objects, but is not strict CSP and does not eliminate every injection scenario.
- Lighthouse is a lab sample; no new visitor tracking was installed and field Core Web Vitals were not measured. Mobile tests emulate browser viewports/touch, not physical iOS/Android hardware. Cross-browser results must be explicitly recorded if run; Chromium alone is not proof for Safari.
- Repository/public-site audit does not certify hosting account security, email delivery, copyright agreements, legal compliance or availability under load.
